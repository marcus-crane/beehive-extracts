27 June 2007

Reappointments of dual members to Historic Places Trust Board and Mâori Heritage Council

  • Judith Tizard
Arts, Culture and Heritage

Hon Judith Tizard, Associate Minister for Arts, Culture and Heritage today announced the reappointment of three members to both the New Zealand Historic Places Trust Board and the Mâori Heritage Council.

The reappointed members are: Tumu te Heuheu is Paramount Chief of Ngati Tuwharetoa and has served one term on both bodies. He is currently Chair of the Maori Heritage Council and also chairs UNESCO’s World Heritage Committee.

Te Aue Davis (Ngati Maniapoto), is a renowned weaver and has worked as a conservationist for over 50 years. She has been a long-term member of the Historic Places Trust Board and the Te Mâori Manaaki Taonga Trust Board.

And Dr Merata Kawharu (Ngati Whatua) is a past tutor in Mâori Studies and is presently a Research Fellow at the James Henare Mâori Research Centre in Auckland. She is an adviser to Te Kawautikitiki Charitable Trust, and a member of the Rhodes Scholarship Selection Committee at the University of Auckland.

The purpose of the Historic Places Trust Board is to promote the identification, protection, preservation and conservation of the historical and cultural heritage of New Zealand.

The Mâori Heritage Council was established under section 84(1) of the Historic Places Act 1993 to ensure that in the protection of wahi tapu, wahi tapu areas, historic places and historic areas of Mâori interest, the New Zealand Historic Places Trust (NZHPT) meets the needs of Mâori in a culturally sensitive manner. The Council assists iwi to develop programmes for identification and conservation of these areas.

For further information contact: 021-227-9871

BACKGROUND INFORMATION

Tumu te Heuheu is Paramount Chief of Tuwharetoa and a member of the Waitangi National Trust Board. He has been chair of Nga Whenua Rahui since its inception in 1990. Tumu was elected chair of UNESCO’s World Heritage Council in 2006. He is completing his first term as Chair of the Mâori Heritage Council.

Te Aue Davis (Ngâti Maniapoto) has been well-known as a weaver and conservationist for over 50 years. She has been a recipient of a Te Waka Toi award for her research into natural cultural resources. A long-term member of the Historic Places Trust Board and the Te Mâori Manaaki Taonga Trust Board, she joined the Nga Whenua Rahui Committee in 2003.

Dr Merata Kawharu (Ngati Whatua) is a past tutor in Mâori Studies and is presently a Research Fellow at the James Henare Mâori Research Centre in Auckland. She established and is an adviser to Te Kawautikitiki Charitable Trust, a member of Te Kaunihera o Te Whare Wananga o Te Pihopatanga o Aotearoa, and a member of the Rhodes Scholarship Selection Committee, University of Auckland.
