Tiz Revue February 2007

  • Judith Tizard
Arts, Culture and Heritage

We’re only just started the year but we’ve already got off to a great start in the arts, culture and heritage sector.

With the Wellington Fringe Festival, the Te Matatini Festival in Palmerston North and AK07 about to kick off in Auckland there is much to celebrate in the arts in New Zealand.

Internationally, New Zealand films have already received acclaim at the Sundance and Berlin Film Festival and kiwi music will be well presented at the South By South West music festival in Texas.

As you can see there are plenty of opportunities to participate in cultural activities – its about making New Zealand a better place to live! Enjoy.
