Minister’s Update

  • Rick Barker
Veterans' Affairs

On 11 November, I took part in a ceremony at the National War Memorial in Wellington to mark the 89th anniversary of the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month of 1918, when hostilities ceased on the Western Front; bringing to a close WWI, a war that took the lives of 18,500 New Zealanders.
