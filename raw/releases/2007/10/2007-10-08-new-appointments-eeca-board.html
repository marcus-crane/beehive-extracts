9 October 2007

New appointments to the EECA board

  • David Parker
Energy

Energy Minister David Parker today announced the appointment of four new members to the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority (EECA).

The Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2000 confers a number of responsibilities on the EECA board including policy development, promoting public awareness of energy efficiency and conservation, arranging for research and demonstrations of technology, monitoring current activities and publishing relevant information.

"I am delighted to appoint and welcome four new board members to the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority for a three year term," David Parker said.

"I congratulate Penelope Hulse, Toni Owen, Andrew Pearce and Gregory Sise on their appointments, and I am pleased to welcome Alastair Patrick to the position of Deputy Chair of the Authority.

"The new members to the board come from various areas of the country and bring a wide range of skills that reach across business, finance, transport and sustainable housing. They bring additional strength in the areas of advocacy, networking and governance.

"The enthusiasm of these board members and our recently appointed chair Roger Sutton will be instrumental in the development of our energy strategies as we work towards achieving our goal of a sustainable New Zealand," David Parker said.

For more information about EECA visit www.eeca.govt.nz

New EECA board members

Toni Owen
Ms Owen is a director of Focus Chartered Accountants Ltd in Whakatane, where she specialises in business and financial planning. Ms Owen has spent over ten years working with communities on Energy and Energy Efficiency. She was trustee of the Eastern Bay Energy Trust from 1995 until 2006, for the last 8 years as Chair. She has played an important role in many organisations in the Eastern Bay of Plenty area including the Child Injury Prevention Trust, the Life Education Trust, and the Chamber of Commerce.

Gregory (Greg) Sise
Mr Sise is the director of Ellsoft Ltd and EnergyLink Ltd in Dunedin, two energy modelling and analysis companies that he founded in1996. Mr Sise has had years of experience working in the energy industry, including with Univord Energy Limited, one of New Zealand’s first energy efficiency consulting companies. He has served on various working groups in the electricity and gas sectors including the New Zealand Electricity Market’s Pricing Working Group and Rules Committee.

Dr Andrew (Andy) Pearce
Dr Pearce is a professional director with specialist governance expertise. He has a diverse range of experience in technology-based companies, commercial and not-for-profit R&D businesses, and charitable organisations. He was CEO of Landcare New Zealand from 1992 until 2005, where he led its formation as an Independent Crown Research Institute and developed it into a profitable company.

Penelope (Penny) Hulse
Ms Hulse has been a Waitakere City Councillor for the last 12 years. Her work there has a special focus on sustainable housing. She has served on the Waitemata District Health Board for the last six years and is trustee of several community trusts including the Earthsong Eco-Neighbourhood Trust, a trust that is designing and building environmentally and socially sustainable homes; and the Waitakere Anti-Violence Essential Services Trust (WAVES), a network of governmental and non-governmental agencies working to prevent family violence and assist the victims of family violence.

Alastair Patrick (Existing member, now appointed as Deputy Chair until 21 June 2008)
Alastair Patrick has been a member of the Authority since June 2005. He is a consultant with experience in transport and transport energy issues. He is currently a contractor for Beacon Consulting Ltd. In this role he provides high level consulting services to government focussing on border security agencies. Between 1991 and 2003 Mr Patrick worked for the Ministry of Transport, for the last three years as manager of Infrastructure and Services.
