6 November 1999

Statement in Response to Media Release by Graham Kelly of 5 November 1999, ‘Nats poised to privatise Housing NZ’

  • Tony Ryall
Housing New Zealand Ltd

"Is it full moon tonight?" asked HNZ Minister, Tony Ryall.

"Labour's Housing Spokesman, Graham Kelly, has really started to lose the plot.

"He is clearly a man under pressure.

"There are no plans to privatise HNZ," said Mr Ryall.
