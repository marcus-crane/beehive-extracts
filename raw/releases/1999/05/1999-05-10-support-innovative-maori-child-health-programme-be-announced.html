10 May 1999

Support For Innovative Maori Child Health Programme To Be Announced

  • Georgina te Heuheu
Associate Minister of Health

The Associate Minister of Health, Hon Georgina te Heuheu will make a pre-Budget announcement concerning the "Tamariki Maraerobics" television programme.

The announcement will be made at 12.30pm Wednesday 12 May 1999, on Papakura Marae, Hunua Rd, Papakura ( 09 2983877)
