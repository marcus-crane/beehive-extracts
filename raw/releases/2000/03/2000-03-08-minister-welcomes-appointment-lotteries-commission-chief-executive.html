8 March 2000

Minister welcomes appointment of Lotteries Commission chief executive

  • Mark Burton
Internal Affairs

Internal Affairs Minister Mark Burton today welcomed the announcement by the Lotteries Commission of the appointment of Arianne Burgess as its new Chief Executive.

Mr Burton noted that Mrs Burgess has an extensive background in general management, marketing and communications and has previously worked as Telecom’s General Manager, Communications.

Mr Burton is particularly pleased that the Lotteries Commission appointment has been finalised with a 35 per cent reduction in the total remuneration package for the new Chief Executive.
