10 August 2005

Minister calls for accuracy in Transport policy debate

  • Pete Hodgson
Climate Change Issues

Transport minister Pete Hodgson says the current Opposition leader Don Brash has made another mistake when talking about transport funding.

"Hot on the heels of the National Party releasing four transport polices in one day on Friday, Dr Brash was on the radio this morning understating the amount this Labour-led government spends on roads," said Pete Hodgson.

Dr Brash declared the government currently spends just $400 million a year on road construction, but the real amount to be spent on roads this year is $1.352 billion.

"Its hard to fathom how the ex-governor of the reserve bank could make such an elemental mistake with his arithmetic," said Pete Hodgson.

"It's not as if he can't get hold of the numbers, after all Land Transport New Zealand had a copy of its plans sent to him and the document is also available on the internet. All he had to do was turn to page 27 and read from the table. How difficult can it be?"

2005/06
Improvement and replacement of state highways $518.20 million
Improvement and replacement of local roads $144.08 million
Maintenance of state highways $355.01 million
Maintenance of Local Roads $335.48 million
Total $1.352 billion
