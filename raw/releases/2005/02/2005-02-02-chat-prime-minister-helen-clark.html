3 February 2005

Chat with Prime Minister Helen Clark

  • Helen Clark
Prime Minister

New Zealanders have the opportunity to chat with Prime Minister Helen Clark
today, Wednesday 2 February, at 5.30pm.

The online chat session - held via the internet and text messaging – will discuss themes and messages from the Prime Minister's speech to the opening of Parliament on 1 February.

This is a great way to have your say, ask questions, and let the Prime Minister know what you think on a range of government initiatives and achievements.

Log on to www.headsup.co.nz and follow the links, or text for free from your mobile phone - text CHAT then a space, then write your comment or question (up to 150 characters) and send to LABR (5227)
