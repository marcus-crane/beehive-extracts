20 April 2005

Associate Health Minister visits Bay of Plenty/ Taupo

  • Damien O'Connor
Health

Associate Health and Rural Affairs Minister Damien O'Connor will meet with local health providers in the Bay of Plenty/Taupo tomorrow and Thursday. He'll also attend a Federated Farmers meeting in Rotorua.

Mr O'Connor said he was looking forward to touching base with rural PHOs and hearing about local issues first hand.

"It's always good to meet with providers on the ground and get a real feel for some of the challenges they face, and what they're achieving."

Mr O'Connor's agenda is below – times are indicative.

Wednesday 20 April 2005

3.30pmMeeting with Waikato PHO providers (Paeroa Medical Centre) including a demonstration of the Rural IT programme.
Venue: Paeroa Medical Centre, 15 Princess Street, Paeroa

4.15pmMeeting with Te Korowai Hauora O Hauraki
Venue: Te Korowai Hauora O Hauraki, 43 Bellmont Street, Paeroa

Thursday 21 April 2005

10.00amTaupo

Meeting with Taupo PHO providers and some members of the Taupo Local Management Group (LMG) members.
Venue: Taupo Medical Centre and Taupo Health Centre, 117 Heu Heu Street Taupo and 113 Heu Heu Street Taupo respectively.

(An LMG is a community based steering group, made up of providers, other community members and Maori, who provide a key link between the community and the Taupo PHO.

1.30pmMangakino

Meeting with Mangakino providers and some Mangakino (LMG) members.
Venue: Board Room, District Council Office, Rangatira Drive, Mangakino

Rotorua
