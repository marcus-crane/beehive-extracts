FBT rate for low interest loans up

The prescribed rate of interest used to calculate fringe benefit tax for low-interest,
employment-related loans will increase on 1 October from 7.5 percent to 7.98 percent, Revenue Minister Michael Cullen announced today.

The rate, which last changed on 1 July this year, is reviewed regularly to align it with the results of the Reserve Bank’s survey of first mortgage rates.
