28 April 2009

Post-Cabinet Press Conference on the Flu Outbreak

  • John Key
  • Tony Ryall
Health Prime Minister

Good afternoon. The process today will be that I'm going to make some comments in relation to the influenza A outbreak that we have, then I'm going to pass over to the Minister and ask him to make some comments and then we will answer any questions you have in relation to that. Then we will take all other questions on other things that we are going to answer. Let me just start with that.
