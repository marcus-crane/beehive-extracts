20 September 2006

Kaikohe Family Start launched by Minister

  • David Benson-Pope
Social Development and Employment

Social Development Minister David Benson-Pope launched the Kaikohe Family Start programme today, and restated the government's commitment to providing comprehensive early intervention services to families.

Run by local PHO Tihewa Mauriora, Family Start in Kaikohe will provide intensive home visits to 112 families with very young children.

The Family Start family/whanau worker helps the family to set and achieve their own goals, acts as a service advocate if necessary, and ensures that services are targeted and well co-ordinated.

Mr Benson-Pope said that Family Start was one of the government's most successful early intervention services, along with well-known initiatives like SKIP, Strengthening Families, and Well Child. Local PHO Tihewa Mauriora was the ideal organisation to deliver Family Start in Kaikohe, with a very strong record of delivering health and wellbeing services to the local community, he said.

Mr Benson-Pope said that an extension to Family Start was part of the government's latest early intervention initiative, Early Years, announced on Monday. Early Years would also establish new service hubs in high-need areas, teen parent co-ordinators in areas with high rates of teenage pregnancy, and a trial Roots of Empathy programme in schools.

"Our goal is to make sure that every family gets the services they need, when they need them," Mr Benson-Pope said. "Early Years is about extending both the reach and the integration of early intervention services like Family Start."
