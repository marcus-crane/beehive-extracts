25 July 2006

Transpower agrees to Connell Wagner recommendations

  • David Parker
Energy

The state-owned transmission company Transpower has agreed to the recommendations in the Connell Wagner report, as summarised by Energy Minister David Parker in a letter to Transpower on 7 July 2006 (10Kb .pdf).

The company's detailed response, dated 21 July 2006, is attached in the clickable link at the head of this document (884Kb .pdf).
