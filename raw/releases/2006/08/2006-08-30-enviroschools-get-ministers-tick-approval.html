31 August 2006

Enviroschools get Minister’s tick of approval

  • David Benson-Pope
Environment

Children from the Wellington region showcasing their schools’ environmental initiatives were congratulated today by Environment Minister David Benson-Pope.

The Minister was at the Wellington region’s first Enviroschools">Ministry for the Environment’s Sustainable Management Fund.

“The Government is committed to maintaining and improving the quality of our environment for now and for our future. This is not something we can achieve alone and having the help and support of these children - our future generation, involved in Enviroschools is really heartening,” said Mr Benson-Pope.
