4 February 2011

Turia congratulates award recipients

  • Tariana Turia
Community and Voluntary Sector

Minister for the Community and Voluntary Tariana Turia has congratulated the recipients of the New Zealander of the Year awards.

520 people and organisations were nominated across the five award categories by individuals or groups who value their outstanding contribution to our country.

“All of the nominees and winners are wonderful ambassadors for New Zealand,” says Mrs Turia.

"It was such an uplifting evening, to be in the company of such champions of the human spirit. Their selfless commitment to their communities and the causes they believe in is truly inspiring".

“I am very proud that former Whanganui resident Sir Paul Callaghan picked up the New Zealander of the Year title.

“His dedication and commitment to the science and business community has been outstanding and will benefit all New Zealanders.”

The winners were:

New Zealander of the Year – Professor Sir Paul Callaghan

Senior New Zealander of the Year - Alison Neill

Young New Zealander of the Year - Jaime Fenton

Local Heroes Award - Billy Graham

Community of the Year - The Gibbston Community Association
