Response to Dominion queries about

  • Sandra Lee
Local Government

The Minister of Local Government declined to comment other than to say if there had been a sin-bin when she was a councillor, she may well have ended up in it.

" Councillors are required to follow Standing Orders set by their respective local authorities on proper behaviour at meetings.

"An effective chair would ensure that any disruption was kept to a minimum."
