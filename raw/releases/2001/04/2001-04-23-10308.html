23 April 2001

Anderton holds first Manufacturing Industry Sector Steering Group meeting

  • Jim Anderton
Industry and Regional Development

ADVISORY

Tuesday 24 April
The first meeting of the Manufacturing Industry Sector Steering Group is to be held tomorrow 24 April 2001.

The meeting has been convened by Deputy Prime Minister Jim Anderton bringing together relevant government agencies ('whole of government' approach) with industry sector representatives. The aim is to identify barriers to the industry creating jobs and building economic activity and work to remove these barriers.

This is the second industry steering group and follows the successful work to date of the Wood Processing Industry Steering Group.

What:Press conference following first meetingf the Manufacturing Industry Sector Steering Group
Who:Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Industry Development Jim Anderton, 14 sector representatives (with another 7 apologies), Ministry of Economic Development, Industry NZ, Trade NZ and Investment NZ officials.
When:Tomorrow, Tuesday 24 April 2001
Time:5 pm
Where:Vogel House, 75 Woburn Rd, Lower Hutt
