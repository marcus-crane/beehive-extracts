30 October 1998

Luxton Welcomes Lifting Of Fruit Ban

  • John Luxton
Food, Fibre, Biosecurity and Border Control

Minister of Food and Fibre, John Luxton, today welcomed China lifting their ban on New Zealand fruit.

"China has huge potential for New Zealand Fruit," Mr Luxton said. "The Lifting of this ban is great news."
